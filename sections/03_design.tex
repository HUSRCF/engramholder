\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}
\paragraph{Predictors and information.}
We adapt frozen Protenix-Mini/Tiny default checkpoints, the official AF2
\texttt{model\_3\_ptm} weights through OpenFold, and AtlasFold with its native
AtlasLM retained. No evaluation homologs, templates, or target structures are
provided as folding inputs. Adapters receive frozen ESM2-35M features (480
channels, layer 12), and Native/Rotated/G+ share this added representation.
The unadapted baseline does not receive the added adapter features; AtlasFold
already has AtlasLM-3B. Thus improvements over the baseline combine feature
access and adaptation, whereas Native--Rotated is the tighter direction control.

\paragraph{Training and pairing.}
The Protenix direction study uses Train24/384 updates, with tangent as the
locked main comparison and Full as a secondary comparison. Full Train96/1536
and Train384/1536 test larger sets at a fixed update budget. OpenFold uses Full
Train96 and Train384, each for 1536 updates; AtlasFold uses Train96/1536.
Within each formal recipe, heads share the data order, training seeds,
random-condition rule, and optimizer settings. Three native seeds are paired
with three seeds for each of three fixed rotations; G+ has three seeds, and the
OpenFold Train96 extension adds nine rotated G+ fits. Checkpoint selection is the
fixed final step, not evaluation quality. One fixed inference sample is scored;
no target-label selection or cropping is used. Training backpropagates through
the final recycle under the native task losses.

\paragraph{Panels and study identity.}
\label{sec:panels}
Confirm96-A supports the historical Factor--Generic comparison; Confirm96-B
supports the original Protenix direction comparison. Both contain 96 short-chain
targets. Length48 comprises 16 targets in each prespecified range 385--512,
513--640 and 641--768; actual lengths are 392--750, beyond the Train384 adapter
training range of 152--383. The original length study locked targets and
configurations before new structure scores. Later OpenFold, AtlasFold and G+
studies reuse observed panels. The OpenFold four-cell Confirm96-B interaction
is the primary comparison of that follow-up, not the project's initial endpoint.
The matched Protenix Train384 G+ follow-up fixes Confirm96-B Native--G+ as
primary and Length48 as secondary; it adds no rotated G+ fits.
Sequence screening, complete-chain mappings and study-specific exclusions are
recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:chronology}; they do not establish family or
foundation-model pretraining isolation.

\paragraph{Scores and uncertainty.}
Our primary score is C$\alpha$ pair-lDDT: for all fixed reference pairs within
15\,\AA, average distance-preservation credit at thresholds 0.5, 1, 2 and
4\,\AA, then give each target equal weight. Missing prediction pairs receive
zero credit without changing the reference denominator. This is a C$\alpha$
pair-averaged variant of lDDT \citep{mariani2013lddt}. Residue-average C$\alpha$
lDDT and fixed-correspondence TM-score \citep{zhang2004tm} are supplementary.
For target $i$ define
\begin{equation}
 d_{H,i}=\frac13\sum_s\left(S_{H,i,s}-\frac13\sum_rS_{H,R_r,i,s}\right),
 \quad \Psi_i=d_{F,i}-d_{G+,i},\quad
 \Psi=\frac1N\sum_i\Psi_i.\label{eq:interaction}
\end{equation}
We resample whole target records for 20,000 paired bootstrap draws, preserving
within-target seed/rotation pairing. Intervals condition on the fitted models;
seeds, rotations and supplementary metrics are not additional independent
proteins. Secondary and follow-up intervals are unadjusted. Protocol-specific
bootstrap seeds and endpoint roles are preserved in the accompanying records.

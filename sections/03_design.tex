\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}
\paragraph{Predictors and information.}
We freeze Protenix-Mini/Tiny default checkpoints, official AF2
\texttt{model\_3\_ptm} weights through OpenFold, and AtlasFold with native
AtlasLM-3B retained. Evaluation inputs exclude homologous sequences,
templates and reference structures; adapter training uses structural
supervision. All four arms within each recipe use the same frozen ESM2-35M features
\citep{lin2023evolutionary} (480 channels, layer 12), or frozen ESMC-600M final-layer features
\citep{candido2026language} (1152 channels; Section~\ref{sec:esmc}).
Unadapted baselines lack these added features, so gains over baseline combine
feature access and adaptation. Within each head, Native--Rotated holds feature
access fixed and measures the effect of output rotation after separate training.
Table~\ref{tab:recipes} summarizes the paths.

\paragraph{Training and pairing.}
The original Protenix direction study trains on Train24 for 384 updates,
with tangent as the prespecified primary comparison and Full as a secondary comparison.
The larger-set studies use Full on Train96 and Train384, with 1536 updates
in each case. OpenFold uses Full on Train96 and Train384, each for 1536 updates;
AtlasFold trains on Train96 for 1536 updates.
Within each core four-cell recipe, heads share data order, three training seeds, random-condition
rules and optimizer settings. Each of three fixed rotations uses all three seeds;
G+ also has three native and nine rotated fits in each core four-cell setting.
The prediction and compensation studies use their separately specified rotation
sets and parameter groups.
We evaluate the fixed final checkpoint and one inference sample, without
target-label selection or cropping. The predictor-specific task losses
backpropagate only through the final recycle in every arm of the core recipes.
The separate Protenix budget follow-up
continues all 24 models to 3072 updates; the OpenFold schedule follow-up
retrains 36 models with one rotation and gradients across four passes
(Appendices~\ref{app:budget_extension} and~\ref{app:schedule_retraining}).

\paragraph{Panels and study identity.}
\label{sec:panels}
Confirm96-A supports the historical Factor--Generic comparison; Confirm96-B
supports the original Protenix direction comparison. Both contain 96 short-chain
targets selected from the existing Protein Data Bank (PDB) structure catalog
\citep{berman2000pdb}: single-model
X-ray entries released by 2021-09-30, resolution at most 2.5\,\AA, standard
amino acids and at least 90\% mapped C$\alpha$ coverage. Four length strata
(128--191, 192--255, 256--319, 320--384) contribute 24 targets each.
Table~\ref{tab:data_composition} distinguishes their construction and first use. Length48 comprises 16 targets in each prespecified range 385--512,
513--640 and 641--768; actual lengths are 392--750, beyond the Train384 adapter
training range of 152--383. The original direction and length studies selected
their evaluation targets before scoring;
later uses of those panels are observed follow-ups. Table~\ref{tab:study_roles}
separates panel novelty from each four-cell study's endpoint role. Fresh192
contains 48 targets per short-chain stratum (actual 128--383 residues), selected
before evaluation of fixed Protenix/ESMC models after updating exclusions
for prior project exposure; its only
primary endpoint is $\Psi$ (Appendix~\ref{app:fresh192}). The channel-compensation
study predeclares mean benefit and gap reduction over two selected rotations;
its original execution and same-seed repeat, the eight-rotation prediction test and Dev8
checkpoint diagnostics retain distinct roles (Appendices~\ref{app:prediction_e1}--\ref{app:e2_compensation}). Earlier
matched-G+ and norm-exchange endpoints remain in their respective appendices.
Train24 $\subset$ Train96 $\subset$ Train384; Train384 has no target-ID
overlap with Dev8 or either confirmation panel. The inherited Train24/Dev8
are not restricted by the new-candidate release cutoff. Sequence screening and
study-specific exclusions are recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:chronology}; they do not establish family or
foundation-model pretraining isolation.

\paragraph{Scores and uncertainty.}
Our primary score is C$\alpha$ pair-lDDT: for all fixed reference pairs within
15\,\AA, average distance-preservation credit at thresholds 0.5, 1, 2 and
4\,\AA, then give each target equal weight. Missing prediction pairs receive
zero credit without changing the reference denominator. This is a C$\alpha$
pair-averaged variant of lDDT \citep{mariani2013lddt}. Residue-average C$\alpha$
lDDT and fixed-correspondence TM-score \citep{zhang2004tm} are supplementary.
For target $i$ define
\begin{equation}
 d_{H,i}=\frac13\sum_s\left(S_{H,i,s}-\frac13\sum_rS_{H,R_r,i,s}\right),
 \quad \Psi_i=d_{F,i}-d_{G+,i},\quad
 \Psi=\frac1N\sum_i\Psi_i.\label{eq:interaction}
\end{equation}
The single-rotation schedule study replaces the inner rotation average by R1.
We write $\Delta_H=N^{-1}\sum_i d_{H,i}$ for the signed Native--Rotated
score difference; positive values favor Native. Thus
$\Psi=\Delta_F-\Delta_{G+}$ compares signed score differences, not absolute rotation
sensitivity. Positive $\Psi$ alone implies neither $\Delta_F>0$ nor
$|\Delta_F|>|\Delta_{G+}|$ and does not rank absolute prediction quality.
We resample whole target records for 20,000 paired bootstrap draws, preserving
within-target seed/rotation pairing; Fresh192 resamples within its four fixed
length strata. Intervals condition on the fitted models;
seeds, rotations and supplementary metrics are not additional independent
proteins. Secondary and follow-up intervals are unadjusted; the OpenFold feature-substitution
study reports its own predeclared three-test Holm correction \citep{holm1979simple}
(Appendix~\ref{app:esmc}). Protocol-specific
bootstrap seeds and endpoint roles are preserved in the accompanying records.

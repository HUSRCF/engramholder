\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}
\paragraph{Predictors and information.}
We freeze Protenix-Mini/Tiny default checkpoints, official AF2
\texttt{model\_3\_ptm} weights through OpenFold, and AtlasFold with native
AtlasLM-3B retained. Evaluation inputs exclude homologous sequences,
templates and reference structures; adapter training uses structural
supervision. All four arms within each recipe use the same frozen
ESM2-35M features \citep{lin2023evolutionary} or ESMC-600M features
\citep{candido2026language}. Unadapted baselines lack these added features,
so their comparison with adapted models combines feature access and
adaptation. Native--Rotated holds feature access fixed within each head.
Appendix Table~\ref{tab:recipes} gives the backbone-specific losses and
inference settings.

\paragraph{Training and pairing.}
Core four-cell recipes use 1536 updates, three training seeds and three
fixed rotations. Each head has three Native and nine Rotated fits.
Within a recipe, arms share data order, seed choices, random-condition
rules and optimizer settings. We evaluate the final checkpoint with one
inference sample per complete target, without selection using evaluation
labels. Task-loss gradients propagate only through the final recycle pass;
the separate injection-schedule study retains gradients across four passes.
The historical Train24 tangent study and intervention-specific training
settings are detailed in Appendices~\ref{app:recipes},
\ref{app:e2_compensation} and~\ref{app:schedule_retraining}.

\paragraph{Panels and study identity.}
\label{sec:panels}
Train24 $\subset$ Train96 $\subset$ Train384, with no target-ID overlap
between Train384 and Dev8 or either Confirm96 panel.
Confirm96-A/B each contain 96 short-chain PDB targets
\citep{berman2000pdb}; Length48 contains 48 complete sequences of
392--750 residues, beyond the Train384 adapter training range of 152--383.
These panels were selected before their original evaluations; later reuse
is reported as observed-panel follow-up. Fresh96 and Fresh192 test fixed
OpenFold and Protenix/ESMC models, respectively, on targets locked before
prediction, each with pair-lDDT $\Psi$ as its sole primary endpoint.
Eligibility criteria, exposure exclusions and study-specific endpoint roles
are in Appendix~\ref{app:chronology} and
Table~\ref{tab:study_roles}. Sequence screening does not establish family
or foundation-model pretraining isolation.

\paragraph{Scores and uncertainty.}
Our primary score is C$\alpha$ pair-lDDT: for fixed reference pairs within
15\,\AA, average distance-preservation credit at thresholds 0.5, 1, 2 and
4\,\AA, then weight targets equally. Missing prediction pairs receive zero
credit with the reference denominator unchanged. This pair-averaged lDDT
variant \citep{mariani2013lddt} differs from supplementary residue-average
C$\alpha$ lDDT; fixed-correspondence TM-score \citep{zhang2004tm} is also
supplementary. For head $H\in\{F,G+\}$, $S_{H,i,s}$ and
$S_{H,R_r,i,s}$ denote Native and rotated scores on target $i$ under
training seed $s$, respectively. Here $R_r$ denotes the $r$th fixed output
rotation. With $N$ targets in the panel, define
\begin{equation}
 d_{H,i}=\frac13\sum_s\left(S_{H,i,s}-\frac13\sum_rS_{H,R_r,i,s}\right),
 \quad \Psi_i=d_{F,i}-d_{G+,i},\quad
 \Psi=\frac1N\sum_i\Psi_i.\label{eq:interaction}
\end{equation}
The single-rotation schedule study uses R1 in place of the inner average.
With $\Delta_H=N^{-1}\sum_i d_{H,i}$,
$\Psi=\Delta_F-\Delta_{G+}$ compares signed score differences.
Positive $\Psi$ alone implies neither $\Delta_F>0$ nor
$|\Delta_F|>|\Delta_{G+}|$ and does not rank absolute prediction quality.
For structure-score contrasts, we use 20,000 paired target-bootstrap draws,
retaining complete within-target seed/rotation records and resampling
within length strata where specified by the protocol. Fresh192 and its
budget extension resample within four fixed length strata. Intervals
condition on fitted models and rotations rather than quantifying
variability over new training runs. Secondary and follow-up confidence
intervals are unadjusted; the OpenFold feature study applies Holm correction
to the $p$ values of three prespecified secondary tests \citep{holm1979simple}
(Appendix~\ref{app:esmc}).

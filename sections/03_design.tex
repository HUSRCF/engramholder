\subsection{Paired contrasts and uncertainty}
For target $i$, form the paired direction contrast by averaging training seeds
and rotations within the target:
\begin{equation}
 d_i=\frac{1}{3}\sum_{s=1}^{3}
 \left(S_{F,i,s}-\frac{1}{3}\sum_{r=1}^{3}S_{F,R_r,i,s}\right).
\end{equation}
The head-by-rotation interaction is
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_i=\left(S_{F,i}-S_{F,R,i}\right)
        -\left(S_{G+,i}-S_{G+,R,i}\right),
 \qquad \Psi=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i\Psi_i,
 \label{eq:interaction}
\end{equation}
where each system score in the last expression is already averaged over its
prespecified seed and rotation instances.

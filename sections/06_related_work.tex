\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
LoRA constrains weight updates \citep{hu2021lora}, while ReFT learns interventions
on frozen hidden representations \citep{wu2024reft}. PiSSA initializes trainable
factors from principal components of pretrained weights and freezes the residual
components \citep{meng2024pissa}. Thus neither representation adaptation nor the
usefulness of pretrained directions is new in itself. We compose residue-level
changes through native pair-generating operators and test their orientation
sensitivity using training-time spectral controls, an absorbable generic head,
and complete-inference norm exchange. Orthogonal finetuning learns transforms,
including butterfly parameterizations \citep{liu2023boft}; our fixed rotations
serve as independently trained controls. AdamW's coordinate-wise optimization
\citep{loshchilov2019adamw} separates function-class equivalence from training
behavior. LoRA-RITE studies optimizer invariance across equivalent low-rank
factorizations of the same weight update \citep{yen2025lorarite}. Our control
instead rotates the completed internal residual relative to a fixed downstream
predictor; Factor lacks a general compensating reparameterization. The related
invariance problem motivates the G+ control, without identifying our effect's
cause.

AlphaFold2/OpenFold, Protenix-Mini and AtlasFold provide distinct tested
computational contexts \citep{jumper2021alphafold,ahdritz2024openfold,gong2025protenix,seo2026atlasfold}.
We preserve their native operators and losses; neither a shared sequence-to-pair
role nor additional baseline scores alone establish a replicated direction effect.

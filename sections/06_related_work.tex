\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
Low-rank weight adaptation \citep{hu2021lora} and learned interventions on hidden
representations \citep{wu2024reft} both exploit a frozen model while restricting
the trainable component. Our construction instead composes residue-level changes
through a pretrained internal pair-generating operator. The contribution is the
controlled comparison of its orientation sensitivity with an absorbable generic
head, not the introduction of activation adaptation or orthogonal
reparameterization itself. Orthogonal finetuning, including butterfly
parameterizations \citep{liu2023boft}, learns transformations for adaptation;
our fixed rotations instead act as trained control conditions for native
operator residuals. Adaptive coordinate-wise optimization
\citep{loshchilov2019adamw} motivates separating function-class equivalence from
training behavior.

AlphaFold2 and its trainable OpenFold implementation couple MSA and pair
representations with structural prediction
\citep{jumper2021alphafold,ahdritz2024openfold}. Protenix-Mini provides compact
folding models with a switchable PLM path \citep{gong2025protenix}; AtlasFold
uses metagenomic-scale language-model representations \citep{seo2026atlasfold}.
These systems provide distinct tested computational contexts. We preserve each
context's native operator and loss rather than equate all sequence-to-pair
interfaces with OPM. Their native system scores locate our adapters' practical
quality, but baseline-only evaluations do not constitute direction-effect
replications.

\section{Related work}
LoRA and PiSSA adapt pretrained weights \citep{hu2021lora,meng2024pissa},
whereas ReFT adapts representations \citep{wu2024reft}. Factor composes
residue-level increments through frozen native pair operators. Orthogonal
finetuning (OFT) and BOFT learn orthogonal transformations
\citep{qiu2023oft,liu2023boft}; our fixed rotations act on completed
residuals, and a separately learned shared map tests compensation.
G+'s rotation closure distinguishes representable functions from
finite-budget training behavior under AdamW \citep{loshchilov2019adamw}.
This distinction complements LoRA-RITE's study of optimization invariance
across equivalent low-rank factorizations \citep{yen2025lorarite}.

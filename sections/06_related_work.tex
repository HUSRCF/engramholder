\section{Related work}
LoRA constrains weight updates \citep{hu2021lora}; ReFT intervenes on frozen
representations \citep{wu2024reft}; PiSSA uses principal components of pretrained
weights \citep{meng2024pissa}. Neither representation adaptation nor pretrained
directions are new in themselves. We compose residue-level changes through
native pair-generating operators and evaluate them with trained spectral
controls and a rotation-closed generic head.

Orthogonal finetuning includes butterfly parameterizations
\citep{liu2023boft}; our novelty is not a learnable orthogonal matrix. The shared
map is an intervention on the cost of fixed training-time rotations.
AdamW's coordinate-wise updates \citep{loshchilov2019adamw} distinguish
function-class closure from training behavior. LoRA-RITE studies optimization
invariance across equivalent low-rank factorizations \citep{yen2025lorarite}.
Here, the completed internal residual is rotated relative to a fixed downstream
predictor, and Factor lacks a general compensating reparameterization.

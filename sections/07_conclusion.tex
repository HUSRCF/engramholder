\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Query-anchored native residuals enable controlled tests of update orientation
in frozen structure predictors. The OpenFold four-cell experiment establishes
an interaction between head parameterization and rotation sensitivity under its
matched recipe; Protenix studies support direction effects and transfer beyond
adapter training lengths. Matched generic-head comparisons favor Native in
Protenix but G+ in some OpenFold settings. Non-supporting OpenFold and AtlasFold
conditions further delimit this result. Full-inference norm exchanges show that
the learned-source structural gap cannot be reduced to global residual scale;
its training origins and detailed downstream pathway remain unresolved.
Preserving native orientation can
help constrained adaptation, but is neither sufficient for gains nor a guarantee
of the best adapter.

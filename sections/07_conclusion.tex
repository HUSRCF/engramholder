\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Four-cell controls establish recipe-dependent signed orientation effects.
Fresh192 provides prospective evidence for the fixed Protenix/ESMC models.
A subsequent budget extension retains a positive interaction on this
now-observed panel while reducing Factor's Native--Rotated score difference.
OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished.
The amended single-rotation OpenFold study finds the largest
interaction at the lowest quality: a larger $\Psi$ need not identify a better
schedule. Compensation improves rotated Factor arms, but their additional
compensation gain relative to Native Factor's gain was not re-established by
same-seed retraining. The prespecified reconstruction diagnostic did not
establish its predicted ranking of post-training Native--Rotated score
differences across eight tested rotations. These limits motivate reporting
absolute performance, signed within-head score differences
and their interaction together under explicit training budgets.

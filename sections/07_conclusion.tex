\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Local spectral matching does not guarantee matching adaptation behavior.
Independently trained four-cell studies establish parameterization-dependent
rotation sensitivity in specified Protenix and OpenFold recipes; Fresh96's
primary interaction remains unestablished. Shared orthogonal compensation
provides useful mitigation in one fixed setting, without validating the failed
reconstruction predictor. Structured-interface evaluation should distinguish
adaptation gains, direction effects, cross-head interactions and head
competitiveness, using output freedom and task training alongside spectral
controls.

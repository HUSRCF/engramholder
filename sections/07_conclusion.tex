\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
At fixed training budgets, output rotation affects Factor and G+
differently in some recipes. Fresh192 supports the Protenix/ESMC
interaction, while OpenFold's Fresh96 and the compensation repeat limit
generalization and mechanistic interpretation. Budget and schedule
follow-ups show why absolute prediction quality, signed within-head
differences, and their interaction should be reported together.

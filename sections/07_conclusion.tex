\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Trained four-cell controls establish parameterization-dependent rotation
sensitivity in specified recipes, including fixed Protenix/ESMC models on
Fresh192; OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished. Shared
orthogonal compensation improves rotated arms, but its additional benefit
over Native is not re-established by same-seed retraining. Reconstruction
does not predict costs. Adaptation gains, direction effects and head
competitiveness therefore require separate evaluation.

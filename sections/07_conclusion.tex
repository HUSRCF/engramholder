\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Complete four-cell studies support parameterization-dependent rotation
sensitivity in specified Protenix and OpenFold recipes on observed panels.
OpenFold's new-target primary interaction remains unestablished, as do the
OpenFold Train384 and AtlasFold interactions. ESMC improves quality without
establishing a change in direction sensitivity. Adapter evaluation should
separately measure useful adaptation, within-head rotation effects and head
competitiveness, retaining a rotation-closed control and direct interactions.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under fixed task-training budgets, four-cell controls establish
parameterization-dependent rotation sensitivity in specified recipes,
including fixed Protenix/ESMC models on
Fresh192, with positive interaction retained after a fixed budget extension;
OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished. Shared
orthogonal compensation improves rotated arms, but its additional benefit
over Native was not re-established by same-seed retraining. A prespecified
reconstruction diagnostic did not establish the predicted ranking of
post-training rotation penalties across eight tested rotations.
Adaptation gains, direction effects and head
competitiveness therefore require separate evaluation.

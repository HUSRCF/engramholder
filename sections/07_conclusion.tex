\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Query-anchored native residuals enable controlled tests of update orientation.
OpenFold Train96/ESM2-35M establishes a head-by-rotation interaction, whereas
Train384 establishes it with neither ESM2 nor ESMC despite higher ESMC scores.
Protenix supports direction effects, length transfer and an advantage over
matched G+; OpenFold's G+ can be better, and AtlasFold bounds the tested scope.
Norm exchanges limit a global-scale explanation without identifying training
origins. Preserving native orientation can help constrained adaptation, but
neither guarantees gains nor determines the best adapter.

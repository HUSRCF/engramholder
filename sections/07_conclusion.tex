\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Query-anchored native residuals enable controlled tests of update orientation
in frozen structure predictors. The OpenFold four-cell experiment establishes
an interaction between head parameterization and rotation sensitivity under its
matched recipe; Protenix studies support direction effects and transfer beyond
adapter training lengths. Generic-head competition and non-supporting OpenFold
and AtlasFold conditions delimit this result. Preserving native orientation can
help constrained adaptation, but is neither sufficient for gains nor a guarantee
of the best adapter.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Fixed Protenix/ESMC models establish the Fresh192 interaction, which
remains positive after the observed-panel budget extension.
OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished.
In a separate OpenFold mechanism study, the compensation repeat improves
rotated Factor without re-establishing a larger gain than Native.
Budget and schedule follow-ups show why absolute prediction quality,
signed within-head differences and their interaction should be reported
together.

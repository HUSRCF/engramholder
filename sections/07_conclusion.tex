\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The fixed Protenix/ESMC recipe establishes a Fresh192 interaction that
persists at the extended budget and in the additional training cohort;
OpenFold's interaction remains unestablished. MSA and CASP16 follow-ups
support adaptation gains under specified caps, without establishing
uniform Factor superiority or gains over full-MSA prediction. The mechanism
remains unresolved. Adapter selection should therefore use absolute
validation quality under the intended inputs and budget, alongside the
signed within-head differences and their interaction.

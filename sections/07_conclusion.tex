\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Four-cell controls establish recipe-dependent signed orientation effects.
Fixed Protenix/ESMC models establish the primary interaction on Fresh192;
OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished. On observed Fresh192,
doubling the budget retains a positive interaction while reducing Factor's
own penalty. The amended single-rotation OpenFold study finds the largest
interaction at the lowest quality: a larger $\Psi$ need not identify a better
schedule. Compensation improves rotated Factor arms, but its extra benefit
over Native was not re-established by same-seed retraining; the reconstruction
diagnostic also failed to establish its predicted penalty ranking. These
limits motivate reporting absolute performance, signed within-head contrasts
and their interaction together under explicit training budgets.

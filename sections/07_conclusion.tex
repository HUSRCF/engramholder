\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
OpenFold Train96/ESM2-35M establishes a head-by-rotation interaction on observed
panels, but Fresh96 does not establish it. Train384 establishes it with neither
ESM2 nor ESMC. Protenix supports direction effects, length transfer and gains
over matched G+; OpenFold's G+ can be better, and AtlasFold bounds the scope.
Adapter evaluation should measure gains and rotation sensitivity separately,
including a rotation-closed control.

\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
Four-cell controls establish parameterization-dependent sensitivity in specified
Protenix and OpenFold recipes; shared orthogonal compensation yields useful
average mitigation within Factor. Neither result predicts direction cost or
guarantees competitiveness: Protenix favors Native over G+, while some OpenFold
comparisons favor G+. Observed-panel support does not replace the unestablished
Fresh96 primary interaction.

Dense rotations, signed permutations and mean-preserving E1/E2 rotations address
distinct explanations. Signed-permutation compatibility with ideal AdamW does
not establish strict FP32 trajectory correspondence.
E2 changes output freedom and joint optimization together; it identifies neither
a learned inverse nor expressivity as the unique cause, and does not establish
elimination of $\Psi$. Across-head controls also change nonlinearities and
residue/pair organization, without proved function-class inclusion.

The eight-rotation teacher-reconstruction predictor failed
($\rho=\result{e1_ca_lddt_x_rho}$, exact one-sided
$p=\result{e1_ca_lddt_x_p}$; Appendix~\ref{app:prediction_e1}). E2 does not rescue
that ranking. Norm exchange constrains global-scale explanations, leaving
content and local magnitudes coupled. Local compatibility, small equal-norm
learned directions and bounded shared-transfer diagnostics retain their
negative boundaries (Appendix~\ref{app:mechanisms}). A separately retrained
comparison with recycle-matched query-only references did not support the
predicted average benefit of live residual anchors to rotated Factor; the two
selected rotations responded in opposite directions
(Appendix~\ref{app:anchor_intervention}). This intervention contains no $C$,
leaving whether $C$'s benefit depends on live anchors untested.

ESMC improves quality without establishing changed direction sensitivity;
family, width, precision and projection size vary together. AtlasFold adds no
established adaptation benefit. Input complementarity and downstream use remain
candidate explanations.

Intervals condition on fitted models and rotations, excluding
retraining and research-selection uncertainty; follow-ups lack joint correction.
Dev8 curves do not establish convergence. Reused panels, one inference sample,
backend differences and incomplete exposure records limit scope.
Sequence screening guarantees neither family nor foundation-model isolation.
Cross-predictor comparisons change losses and interfaces; gaps to native
single-sequence systems limit deployment claims. Evaluation should consider
output freedom and task training alongside local spectra under explicit budgets.

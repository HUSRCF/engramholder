\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
The complete controls distinguish adaptation gains, within-head direction
effects and cross-head interactions. Protenix's Train96 follow-up extends
positive interaction evidence beyond OpenFold, and Train384 retains it with
both feature sources. These are observed-panel studies: they do not replace
OpenFold's unestablished Fresh96 primary interaction. OpenFold Train384 and
AtlasFold further limit generality. The interaction compares whole head
architectures, without isolating the fixed decoder, function-class constraints
or optimization geometry. No inclusion of Factor's function class within G+
has been proved. Matched comparisons favor Native in Protenix and G+ in some
OpenFold settings; rotation closure alone does not make a head preferable.

ESMC raises Protenix and OpenFold structural scores, but direct ESMC--ESM2
changes in $\Delta_F$ and $\Psi$ remain uncertain. PLM family, width, cache
precision and input-projection size change together, with backend differences
in specified comparisons. AtlasFold retains its native AtlasLM and establishes
no useful added adaptation with either feature source. High baseline quality,
redundant information and downstream use are candidate explanations, not
isolated causes. A prospective diagnostic across eight locked OpenFold rotations
did not find that finite-budget shared teacher-update reconstruction error
predicts retrained direction cost ($\rho=\result{e1_ca_lddt_x_rho}$,
exact one-sided $p=\result{e1_ca_lddt_x_p}$; Appendix~\ref{app:prediction_e1}).
This limits that diagnostic as a selection rule; input complementarity and
downstream propagation remain unvalidated candidates.

The norm exchange limits an overall-scale explanation of one Protenix gap,
without separating learned content, local magnitudes and channel organization.
Local diagnostics retain their negative boundaries: oracle compatibility did
not establish a positive association with folding gains, small equal-norm
learned directions showed no established native advantage, and bounded
Train8-to-Dev8 updates showed no established mean positive transfer. These
recipes and endpoints differ from complete inference; beneficial residuals'
training origin remains unresolved (Appendix~\ref{app:mechanisms}).

Intervals condition on fitted models, excluding full retraining and
research-selection uncertainty. Follow-up intervals are not jointly adjusted.
Convergence at 1536 updates is unestablished. Three seeds and rotations cover
limited instances; panels are reused and one inference sample does not estimate
sampling variability. Sequence screening guarantees neither family nor
pretraining isolation; historical exposure remains incomplete. Cross-predictor
comparisons also change interfaces, representations and losses. Gaps to strong
native single-sequence systems limit deployment claims. These findings delimit
a conditional design factor, not a universal adapter prescription.

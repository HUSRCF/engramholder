\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
The Protenix/ESMC interaction persists across two tested budgets;
OpenFold's Fresh96 interaction remains unestablished. Neither result
establishes convergence or a universal parameterization effect. First-only
injection gives the largest interaction but the lowest quality, motivating
joint reporting of $\Psi$, absolute scores and both within-head differences.

Compensation benefits rotated Factor in both executions, but its additional
gain over Native was not re-established in the repeat. Output freedom and joint
optimization remain coupled, and the schedule comparison changes training
and inference together. These interventions do not identify a unique
mechanism. Reconstruction prediction and the residual-anchor intervention
also failed to establish their proposed effects
(Appendices~\ref{app:prediction_e1} and~\ref{app:anchor_intervention}).

Target intervals condition on fitted models and rotations, excluding
variability across new training runs. The same-seed repeat adds no
independent seeds or retrained no-$C$ controls; its executions are not
pooled. Different interval significance does not establish a
between-execution population difference. Follow-ups have no joint
multiplicity correction; the OpenFold feature study retains its
prespecified Holm correction.

AtlasFold showed no established adaptation benefit. Limited budgets, one
inference sample, differing interfaces, losses and backends, and incomplete
exposure records restrict generalization. Sequence screening establishes
neither family nor foundation-model pretraining isolation. Gaps to native
single-sequence systems limit deployment claims.

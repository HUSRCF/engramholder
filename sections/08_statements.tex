\subsection*{AI use statement}
AI assistants supported code development, experiment orchestration, analysis
checks, and manuscript drafting. The authors checked the numerical results
against experimental records and remain responsible for the content.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies computational adaptation using protein structure data. Its
results concern the stated structure-prediction settings and do not validate
biological function, clinical use, or safety of designed proteins. Data and model
access remain subject to their respective source terms.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The accompanying materials provide adapter implementations, training and
scoring configurations, per-target scores, and code to reproduce the tables
and figures. Appendices~\ref{app:recipes}, \ref{app:chronology} and
\ref{app:scoring} describe the model-specific recipes, study sequence and
statistical analysis. The materials also include a one-target OpenFold
inference example with feature extraction and scoring, and CPU operator
examples using synthetic inputs. These examples do not reproduce the full
training and evaluation matrix. Pretrained model weights, the full structure
data and an end-to-end retraining environment are not bundled; setup
instructions and implementation checks accompany the code.

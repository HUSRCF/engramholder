\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies computational adaptation using protein structure data. Its
results concern the stated structure-prediction settings and do not validate
biological function, clinical use, or safety of designed proteins. Data and model
access remain subject to their respective source terms.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The accompanying materials provide adapter source snapshots, training and
scoring recipe summaries, per-target scores, and code to regenerate numerical
results and plots from archived scores. Appendices~\ref{app:recipes},
\ref{app:chronology} and~\ref{app:scoring} describe the model-specific recipes,
study sequence and statistical analysis. A one-target OpenFold example
includes a trained adapter, feature extraction, prediction and scoring;
prediction requires separately obtained upstream software and foundation-model
weights. CPU operator examples use synthetic inputs. The package does not
reproduce the full training and evaluation matrix: complete training data,
feature caches, the full set of trained adapters and predicted structures,
and an end-to-end retraining environment are not bundled.

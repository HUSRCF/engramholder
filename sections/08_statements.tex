\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies computational adaptation using protein structure data. Its
results concern the stated structure-prediction settings and do not validate
biological function, clinical use, or safety of designed proteins. Data and model
access remain subject to their respective source terms. The final submission
requires the authors' review of the applicable provenance and use conditions.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
The accompanying analysis artifact reconstructs manuscript tables and figures
from fixed score records and includes numerical source mappings. It also provides
a bounded operator-level reproduction entry point. Appendix~\ref{app:recipes}
describes the backbone-specific recipes, Appendix~\ref{app:chronology} the study
sequence, and Appendix~\ref{app:scoring} the metrics and pairing. This compact
artifact does not bundle pretrained model weights, the original full structure
data, or an end-to-end retraining environment. A clean-environment route from
sequence and released adapter weights to a full prediction is not yet validated
in this compact package. A personal development repository
is not an anonymous submission artifact; the prepared archive must be checked
before upload.

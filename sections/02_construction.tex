\subsection{Query-anchored residual adaptation}
\label{sec:residual}
Let $x$ denote the query sequence, $e(x)$ frozen sequence features, and
$f_\theta$ a trainable shared predictor. At interface $k$ and recycle $t$,
write the native factors as $a_{k,t},b_{k,t}$ and the baseline update as
$U_{k,t,0}$. Define
\begin{align}
 (\delta a,\delta b)&=f_\theta(e(x)),\label{eq:increments}\\
 \Delta U_{k,t}
 &=G_{k,t}(a_{k,t}+\delta a,b_{k,t}+\delta b)
   -G_{k,t}(a_{k,t},b_{k,t}),\label{eq:residual}\\
 U^{(R)}_{k,t}&=U_{k,t,0}+\mathcal R_k\Delta U_{k,t}.\label{eq:injection}
\end{align}

\subsection{A generic head that can absorb the rotation}
\label{sec:generic}
For a generic residual with a free final affine layer,
\begin{equation}
 \Delta U_G=Wh_\phi+b,\qquad
 \Delta U_{G,R}=R(W_Rh_\phi+b_R).
\end{equation}
Taking $W_R=R^\top W$ and $b_R=R^\top b$ gives
$\Delta U_{G,R}=\Delta U_G$ and hence $\mathcal F_G^R=\mathcal F_G$.

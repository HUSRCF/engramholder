\subsection{Four-cell results with additional training seeds}
\label{app:followup_seeds}

We retrained the query-only Protenix/Train384/ESMC and
OpenFold/Train96/ESM2 configurations with three additional training seeds
per predictor. Each cohort used the original three fixed rotations and
1,536 updates per fit: two heads, each with a Native fit and three
Rotated fits per training seed, gave 24 fits per predictor. We evaluated
the new models on the previously evaluated Fresh192 and Fresh96 panels,
respectively (Appendices~\ref{app:fresh192} and~\ref{app:fresh96}).
The new cohorts used separate execution environments from the original
fits and are reported separately.

All Protenix cells used the same three fixed inference seeds, with one
structure sample per seed and target. We formed paired contrasts within
each inference seed and averaged them within target and training seed.
OpenFold used deterministic query-only inference. The 95\% intervals
in Table~\ref{tab:followup_seeds} use 20,000 paired target-bootstrap
draws, within the four fixed length strata for Protenix and over the
full panel for OpenFold. They condition on the fitted models, the three
fixed rotations, and the inference seeds where applicable.

All three additional Protenix training seeds had positive interaction
point estimates. Separately, holding each Protenix inference seed fixed
and averaging over training seeds and rotations gave
$\Psi=\rebuttalProtenixInferenceMeans$. The OpenFold cohort mean
remained inconclusive. Exploratory intervals that resample both targets
and training seeds were $\rebuttalProtenixSeedInterval$ for Protenix
and $\rebuttalOpenFoldSeedInterval$ for OpenFold. The results provide
evidence across additional training runs and the tested inference
samples for the Protenix recipe. These analyses do not estimate
uncertainty for a new random rotation.

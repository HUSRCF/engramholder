\subsection{Predicting new rotations from initialization gradients}
\label{app:rotation_prediction}

This study tested whether the actual shared Factor head's task gradients
could predict final structure-score differences before full retraining.
It retained the query-only Protenix Mini-default/Train384/ESMC recipe and
1,536 updates. Train24 and the disjoint Dev8 supplied the predictor;
Fresh192 was already observed and supplied the final structure evaluation.
The prediction phase used no Fresh192 scores or reference structures.

\paragraph{Candidate family and predictor.}
We generated 64 rotations in eight fixed groups of eight, all in
$SO(128)$ with the same complete eigenangle spectrum, Frobenius distance
16 from identity, and the same left and right action on the channel-mean
direction. This is a restricted rotation family, not arbitrary Haar sampling.
At zero-residual initialization, only the head's final shared affine layer
has nonzero parameter gradients. For each rotation, each Train24 target's
gradient generated one virtual first AdamW update under the original recipe.
We averaged these parameter increments across training targets and took
their negative inner product with the mean Dev8 gradient:
\[
q(R)=-\left\langle\overline{g}_{\mathrm{Dev8}}(R),
                    \overline{\delta\theta}_{\mathrm{Train24}}(R)\right\rangle.
\]
The update is computed per target before averaging; this is not AdamW
applied to the mean gradient. We averaged $q$ over three head initialization
seeds and three task-noise seeds, retaining the original recycle-gradient
truncation. The predictor uses shared head parameters, not independently
fitted per-target residue factors.

Within each group, the largest and smallest $q$ were labeled predicted
good and bad, with candidate-order tie breaking. The prespecified gate
required at least six of eight pairs to retain this order in at least
two of three initialization marginals and two of three noise marginals,
alongside gradient and virtual-update checks. All eight pairs passed;
all were retained. This development gate is not evidence of successful
prediction of final structure scores. The 16 selected rotations and
analysis were fixed before their full-training structure predictions.

\paragraph{Validation and estimand.}
Eight pairs, two rotations per pair, two heads and three paired training
seeds gave 96 fits. Three paired inference seeds on 192 targets gave
55,296 predictions. For each head $H$, let
$D_H=\operatorname{mean}_{\mathrm{pairs}}(S_{H,\mathrm{good}}-
S_{H,\mathrm{bad}})$ after averaging targets and training/inference seeds.
The primary contrast is $T=D_F-D_{G+}$. Equivalently, $T$ is the difference
between the bad-rotation and good-rotation interactions because the Native
terms cancel; the primary test requires no new Native fits. Predictive
support requires both $D_F$ and $T$ to have positive estimates and primary
intervals above zero. Labels are not reversed after observing scores.

The primary 95\% intervals use 20,000 crossed paired bootstrap draws over
rotation groups and targets within the four fixed Fresh192 length strata,
preserving head, training-seed and inference-seed pairing. They condition
on the three training and inference seeds. Additional training-seed
resampling is exploratory. The eight selected pairs support inference
only within this candidate-generation and selection procedure.

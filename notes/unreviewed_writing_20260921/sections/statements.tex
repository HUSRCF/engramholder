\section*{AI use statement --- working disclosure}
Generative AI assistants were used to assist with research planning, code
implementation, experiment operations, numerical analysis, literature lookup
and manuscript drafting. This disclosure is a working draft: the authors must
verify the complete account of tool use and document their own review before
submission. No claim that all AI-assisted material has already received human
verification is made in this working manuscript.

\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The appendix documents the implemented operators, inference and loss contracts,
training and target-selection chronology, numerical audits and unsuccessful
follow-ups. Fixed weight identities, source snapshots, model--target scores and
execution locks are retained with the experiment artifacts. The accompanying
writing snapshot includes the numerical summaries used by the main tables;
a release location for the complete anonymized artifact is pending. Results
from incomplete adapter matrices are explicitly marked pending and excluded
from the paper's scientific conclusions.
